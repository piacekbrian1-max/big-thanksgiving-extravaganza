The trip brain

Tell it where you are based.

Give the planner your hotel, address or neighborhood. If you leave it blank, it uses downtown / the city center as the working home base. It then clusters each day around that base instead of scattering random attractions across the city.

The planner will use your saved interests, must-sees, traveler profile, dates, food preferences and home base. It also checks date-specific events where destination data is available.

Smart itinerary

What should we do each day?

Each day is built as a geographic cluster with a morning, afternoon and evening. You can edit every suggestion, replace individual ideas, or rebuild the day after changing your home base.

Local guide

Food, drinks & things to do.

Suggestions are organized by meal, neighborhood, entertainment and interest instead of one long tourist list.
